## The Hundred-Hour Problem

I've watched it happen dozens of times. A marketing director realizes their organic traffic is collapsing. They call a meeting. The SEO team runs their usual audits — keywords, backlinks, page speed. The dev team spends weeks restructuring meta tags. A consultant charges $15,000 to write a "content strategy."

Three months and hundreds of billable hours later, nothing changes. Because none of them were solving the actual problem.

**The problem isn't SEO. The problem is AEO.**

Answer Engine Optimization is fundamentally different from Search Engine Optimization. SEO is about ranking on a list. AEO is about being the source an AI cites when it gives a direct answer. And the technical requirements are completely different:

- **Structured Data (JSON-LD):** AI agents need machine-readable "business cards" — Schema.org markup that tells them exactly what you do, what you offer, and why you're authoritative.
- **Semantic HTML:** AI parses your content hierarchy. Generic `<div>` soup is invisible; proper `<article>`, `<section>`, and `<main>` tags are readable.
- **Factual Density:** AI prefers specific data points — pricing, specs, service areas — over marketing fluff. "Communication Solutions Simplified" tells an AI nothing. "VoIP provider serving 12 states with 99.9% uptime" tells it everything.
- **Entity Authority (E-E-A-T):** AI checks if your claims are verifiable. Named authors, structured reviews, and links to authoritative third-party sources build trust.
- **Citation Probability:** The likelihood that an AI will attribute a specific answer to your website instead of a competitor's.

Your 10-person web team would need to manually audit every one of these dimensions across your entire site. They'd need to understand how multiple AI engines — each with different parsing behaviors — interpret your content. They'd need to generate custom Schema.org markup, rewrite content for factual density, restructure your HTML, and validate the results.

Conservatively? That's 200-400 hours of specialized work. At agency rates, you're looking at $30,000-$60,000.
