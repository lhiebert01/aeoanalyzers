### What You Actually Get: The Action Plan Inside Every Citation Sweep

Your customers have started asking ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude who to buy from. Most businesses have never seen those answers. Some are being recommended. Most are absent — and a competitor is being named instead.

Plenty of tools will now tell you *that* this is happening. They count your mentions, chart your visibility, and leave you with a dashboard and a question: okay — what do I actually do?

That question is what AEO Analyzers is built to answer. A Citation Sweep doesn't end at a score. It ends at an action plan with five layers, each one answering "what do I change, and where?"

**1. Code fixes your web team can paste in.** The engines read structure before they read prose, so your report starts with the machine-readable groundwork: verified JSON-LD schema written for your pages, with a clear line between what's confirmed working and what's a candidate to add — packaged as a roadmap plus a handoff email.

**2. Corrections for what AI gets wrong about you.** Every branded answer is checked against your site's own record and classified: accurate, drifted from the facts, or confused with another entity entirely. We built this because it happened to us — an engine once invented a co-founder for this company. We found it, traced it, fixed it, and turned the method into a feature.

**3. An off-site plan built from real citations.** The sources engines actually lean on in your category, in priority order — first-party and legitimate only. We document the gray-hat crash risk rather than sell it.

**4. A battle-map of the questions that matter.** Real buyer questions, multiple times each, across all four engines, with the sample size and confidence printed next to every number — and which competitors are recommended in your place, by name.

**5. Proof that the fixes worked.** Every number is backed by a stored transcript. Run the same question yourself and you'll get what the report says. Then re-sweep and compare — measured before, measured after.

We ran this on ourselves 240 times. AI finds us by name 98% of the time. On unbranded buyer questions, it recommends us 0% of the time — and we're showing you every number, because a tool that measures AI visibility should be able to take its own measurement.
