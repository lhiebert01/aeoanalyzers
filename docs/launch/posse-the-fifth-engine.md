## Social versions (POSSE: publish here first, then link)

### LinkedIn

Our Citation Sweeps now ask a fifth AI engine. Pro and Business plans add Grok to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini.

The interesting part is not the engine. It is what happens to the number when you change engines. On our own site, adding one engine to the same answers once moved the category recommendation rate by 14 points, with nothing on the website changing. So the five-engine panel starts its own series, and our monthly figures stay on four engines so each month compares with the last.

We also explain why Google's AI Overviews, AI Mode and Copilot are not in the panel: none of them has an API that returns its answer, so no one can ask them the same question under the same conditions twice.

And running it on ourselves as a customer turned up a real finding: an engine described us with last week's prices. The report now catches that.
