### Six Advanced Analysis Features That No Other Tool Has

This is where AEO Analyzers v1.2 leaves every other platform behind. Every analysis now includes six deep-dive diagnostics that previously required an entire consulting engagement to produce.

#### 1. Citation Hook Density

AI doesn't cite pages that "feel" authoritative. It cites pages with **specific, quotable facts.**

Citation Hook Density counts every statistic, percentage, and protocol on your page. It gives you a factual density score (0-100) and — critically — **surfaces the top 3 most citable sentences** from your content.

If you have zero stats and zero percentages? Your density score tells you exactly why AI skips you.

#### 2. E-E-A-T Author Audit

Google's E-E-A-T framework (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) directly affects whether AI trusts your content enough to cite it.

Our E-E-A-T audit checks:
- Does your page have a named human author?
- Is the author generic ("Admin", "Staff", "Team")?
- Are there Schema.org/Person tags, LinkedIn links, credentials?
- What specific trust signals exist — and which are missing?

If your homepage says "Written by Admin" — or worse, has no author at all — your E-E-A-T score tells you exactly how much trust you're leaving on the table.

#### 3. LLM Summarization Test

Here's a question no other tool asks: **Does AI actually understand what your page is about?**

The LLM Summarization Test compares what your meta title and description *say* the page is about versus what AI *actually summarizes* from the content. The result is one of three ratings:

- **Aligned:** Your metadata matches reality. AI gets it.
- **Vague:** AI understands you, but your metadata doesn't commit to anything specific.
- **Misaligned:** Your metadata promises one thing. Your content delivers another. AI is confused.

This is the single most overlooked problem in AEO. Most businesses have metadata written for humans and content written for... also humans. Neither is optimized for AI comprehension.

#### 4. Zero-Click / Featured Snippet Predictor

When someone asks Google a question and gets an answer *without clicking*, that's a zero-click result. The content that appears in that answer box? It's almost always formatted as a **table, list, or definition.**

The Zero-Click Predictor scans your content and identifies text blocks that *should* be tables or lists but aren't. It tells you exactly what to restructure and why — with a snippet readiness score (0-100).

If your competitor's content is in a table and yours is in a paragraph, they win the featured snippet. Every time.

#### 5. Query-to-Content Gap

This is the feature that makes marketing directors sit up straight.

AEO Analyzers generates the **top 10 questions** that users in your niche would actually ask an AI. Then it checks whether your content answers them. Each question is rated:

- **Strong:** Your content directly and clearly answers this question.
- **Partial:** There's something relevant, but it's vague or incomplete.
- **Missing:** Your content doesn't address this at all.

A gap score of 30/100 means 70% of the questions your customers are asking AI about your industry — *your content doesn't answer.* That's 70% of conversations where a competitor gets cited instead.

#### 6. Semantic Chunking Analysis

AI engines don't read walls of text. They read *chunks* — content blocks with clear headings that can be extracted and cited independently.

Semantic Chunking finds every content block over 150 words that lacks a proper heading. For each one, it suggests a specific heading and explains the context. The chunking score tells you how well your content is structured for AI extraction.

A score of 100 means AI can cleanly pull any section from your page. A score of 30 means most of your content is a monolithic block that AI will struggle to parse.
