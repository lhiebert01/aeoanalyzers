**The diagnosis (free):**
- **0–100 AEO score** across four dimensions — Entity, Density, Clarity, Structure.
- **Citation Probability** — how likely AI is to cite you when answering questions in your domain.
- **Competitive Duel** — head-to-head vs any competitor; see who AI would name.
- **Brand-type detection** — editorial / news / SaaS / ecommerce / service (scoring adapts to your type).

**The cure (the part most "AI audit" tools don't give you):**
- **Paste-ready JSON-LD**, split into **Verified** (detected on your page) vs **Candidate** (verify before publishing) — *so you never paste a guessed "fact" that teaches AI something wrong about you.*
