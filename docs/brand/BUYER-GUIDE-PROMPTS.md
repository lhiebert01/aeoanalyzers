# BUYER GUIDE / LONG-FORM — image prompts (brand-updated)

**Replaces the appendix in `AEO_Analyzers_Buyers_Standard_LONG_Form_Series.docx`.**
Six images: hero 1600×900 and OG 1200×630 for each of the three long-form pieces.

## What changed, and why

The original appendix specified **ink-teal `#08343B` / `#071A1E`, bright teal `#2DD4BF`,
cream `#F4F1EA`**, plus "simple flat icons". None of that is the brand. Colours sampled
directly from the shipped asset `aeoanalyzers-og-brand.png`: background **`#000603`**,
signal **`#68E66D` green — not teal**, type **`#FFFFFF`**. The shipped card uses **no
pictographic icons**; its motif is the data-bar.

Two further changes of substance, not style:

- **"Cream serif headline" is dropped.** The brand headline face is a heavy geometric
  grotesque, not a serif. The serif belongs to the blog body type, not to cards.
- **Long 3's footer vendor list is removed.** The original put "Profound · Peec AI ·
  OtterlyAI · Scrunch · AthenaHQ · Rankscale · SE Ranking · AEO Analyzers" along the
  bottom of the OG card. Naming seven competitors inside an image we publish, where
  nothing is dated and nothing can be corrected, is a claim we cannot maintain — and the
  card would be screenshotted long after the page beneath it was updated. The page names
  them, with dates and sources. The card should not.

## Palette — five hues, each with a job

These are the product's own semantic colours, lifted for a dark background and measured
rather than assumed. A reader who has used the tool already knows what they mean.

| Role | Hex | Use for | Contrast on bg |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | `#000603` | the field, with a subtle radial lift toward centre | — |
| Positive / present | `#68E66D` | filled bars, arcs, confirmed states — the dominant colour | 12.8:1 |
| Warn / partial | `#E8B23C` | partial fills, "depends on tier", incomplete | 10.6:1 |
| Gap / missing | `#F2705C` | absent, unpublished, not assessed | 7.1:1 |
| Neutral | `#9DB2B7` | inert structure, grids, unlabelled competitor shapes | 9.2:1 |
| Type | `#FFFFFF` | headlines and labels; 70% for secondary | 20.4:1 |

All AAA. **Green must dominate** — it should read as a green image at a glance; amber and
clay are accents that carry meaning, never filler. **No blue, no teal.** Blue is the
product-marketing system (navy `#00081B`, blue `#0056F0`, CTA button, phone mockup) and
mixing the two makes both look accidental.

## System block — paste at the top of every prompt

> **Type:** heavy geometric grotesque headlines, tight tracking, sentence case. Labels and
> eyebrows UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing (~0.18em), small, white.
> **Graphic language — use it, do not minimise it:** dimensional cards with soft shadow
> and a 1px coloured edge; outlined bars with partial fill and a visible numeric value; a
> circular score ring with an arc and a large white numeral; fragments of answer-transcript
> text set small and dim, suggesting the evidence behind a number; thin connector lines;
> crisp geometric grids. Detail and density are wanted.
> **Depth allowed:** soft drop shadows, gentle radial vignette, one plane above another.
> **Not allowed:** neon glow, bevels, 3-D extrusion, lens flare, photography, stock imagery.
> **Never:** shields, padlocks, circuit boards, robot faces, brains, magnifying glasses,
> speedometers, podiums, trophies, stars, rating badges, competitor logos or names in the
> artwork, or any call-to-action button.
> **Required furniture:** eyebrow top-left UPPERCASE letterspaced white; thin green hairline
> across the lower third; `AEO ANALYZERS` lower-left and `AEOANALYZERS.COM` lower-right,
> UPPERCASE letterspaced white, small.
> **Text discipline:** every rendered word spelled exactly as written. Readable at thumbnail
> size. Nothing decorative behind type.

## Production notes

- **Proof every rendered word.** Generators mangle text. If a headline returns with a typo,
  keep the illustration and set the type yourself — do not re-roll and hope.
- **Export opaque RGB, no alpha channel.** A PNG with transparency renders black-on-black in
  some feeds. Inspect the channel; do not judge by eye.
- **Never upscale.** Generate at or above final size and downscale only. JPEG quality 95 at
  4:4:4 chroma, or PNG-24 for flat vector.
- **Check the OG at quarter size** before publishing. If the sub-line is unreadable as a feed
  thumbnail, the card has failed however good it looks full size.
- **Do not tint any colour toward the background** to soften it. Every value above is at its
  measured contrast for a reason.
