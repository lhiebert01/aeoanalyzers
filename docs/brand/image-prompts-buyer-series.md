# Image prompts — AEO Buyer series (brand-compliant)

Derived from the shipped brand asset `aeoanalyzers-og-brand.png`, colours sampled from
the file rather than specified from memory. Supersedes the teal-palette draft: the brand
is near-black and **green**, not ink-teal, and it uses **no pictographic icons**.

## Brand system (paste this block at the top of every prompt)

> **Palette, exact:** background near-black `#000603`. Signal colour bright green
> `#68E66D`. Type pure white `#FFFFFF`. Secondary type white at 70% opacity. No other
> colours.
> **Type:** heavy geometric grotesque for headlines, tight tracking, sentence case.
> Labels and eyebrows in UPPERCASE with wide letter-spacing (~0.18em), small, white.
> **Motif:** thin green-outlined rectangles with partial green fill — the data-bar. Thin
> green hairline rules. Plain geometric shapes and numerals.
> **Forbidden:** gradients, glows, drop shadows, 3-D, photography, stock imagery,
> shields, padlocks, circuit boards, robot faces, brains, magnifying glasses, gauges,
> speedometers, podiums, trophies, stars, rating badges, competitor logos, and any
> pictographic icon at all.
> **Required furniture:** eyebrow top-left in uppercase letterspaced white. Thin green
> hairline rule across the lower third. `AEO ANALYZERS` lower-left and
> `AEOANALYZERS.COM` lower-right, both uppercase letterspaced white, small.
> **Text discipline:** every word rendered must be spelled exactly as given. Large,
> high-contrast, readable at thumbnail size. Nothing decorative behind type.

## Production notes

- **Proof every rendered word.** Generators mangle text. If a headline comes back with a
  typo, keep the illustration and set the type yourself — do not re-roll and hope.
- **Export opaque RGB, no alpha channel.** A PNG with transparency renders black-on-black
  in some feeds. Check the channel, do not judge by eye.
- **JPEG quality 95, 4:4:4 chroma** for photographic-weight files; PNG-24 is fine for
  flat vector. Never upscale — generate at or above final size and downscale only.
- **Contrast floor.** White on `#000603` is ~19:1; green `#68E66D` on `#000603` is ~11:1.
  Both clear AAA. Do not tint either toward the background to make it "softer".
- **Check the OG card at 1/4 size** before publishing. If the sub-line is unreadable in a
  feed thumbnail, the card has failed regardless of how it looks at full size.
