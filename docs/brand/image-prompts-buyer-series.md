# Image prompts — AEO Buyer series (brand-compliant)

Derived from the shipped brand asset `aeoanalyzers-og-brand.png`, colours sampled from
the file rather than specified from memory. Supersedes the teal-palette draft: the brand
is near-black and **green**, not ink-teal, and it uses **no pictographic icons**.

## Which palette, and why

Two approved systems exist in `gbp-assets/aeoanalyzers`, both sampled from the files:

| | **A — Editorial mono** | **B — Product marketing** |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#000603` near-black | `#00081B` deep navy |
| Primary | green `#68E66D` | blue `#0056F0`, deep `#0032A8` |
| Accent | — | success green `#369959` |
| Signature | data-bars, flat, no pictograms | device mockup, score ring, UI cards, radial glow, CTA |

**This series uses A.** Not on taste — on argument. System B is product advertising: it
carries a call-to-action button, a phone mockup and a logo mark. On a page whose entire
claim is that it is an impartial buyer's guide naming no winner, an advert-shaped image
contradicts the text before anyone reads it. System A reads as reference material.

**Editorial does not mean empty.** Every image below carries a real hero object with
depth, data and detail. Sparse is not the goal; *unbranded-looking* is not the goal.
The goal is that it looks like something a buyer would screenshot into a procurement
deck rather than something a vendor would run as an ad.

## Brand system (paste this block at the top of every prompt)

> **Palette — five hues, each with a job.** These are the product's own semantic colours
> lifted for a dark background, not decoration, so a reader who has used the tool already
> knows what they mean.
>
> | Role | Hex | Use it for | Contrast on bg |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Background | `#000603` | the field, with a subtle radial lift toward centre | — |
> | Positive / present | `#68E66D` | the primary signal: filled bars, arcs, confirmed states | 12.8:1 |
> | Warn / partial | `#E8B23C` | partial fills, "depends on tier", incomplete states | 10.6:1 |
> | Gap / missing | `#F2705C` | absent, unpublished, not assessed | 7.1:1 |
> | Neutral | `#9DB2B7` | inert structure, grids, unlabelled competitor shapes | 9.2:1 |
> | Type | `#FFFFFF` | all headline and label text; 70% for secondary | 20.4:1 |
>
> Green stays dominant — it should read as a green image at a glance. Amber and clay are
> accents that carry meaning, never filler. **No blue and no teal anywhere:** blue is the
> product-marketing system and mixing the two makes both look accidental.
> **Type:** heavy geometric grotesque for headlines, tight tracking, sentence case.
> Labels and eyebrows UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing (~0.18em), small, white.
> **Graphic language — use it, do not minimise it:** dimensional cards with soft
> shadow and a 1px green edge; green-outlined bars with partial fill and a visible
> numeric value; a circular score ring with a green arc and a large white numeral;
> fragments of answer-transcript text set small and dim, suggesting evidence behind a
> number; thin green connector lines; crisp geometric grids. Detail and density are
> wanted.
> **Depth is allowed:** soft drop shadows, a gentle radial vignette, one plane sitting
> above another. **Not allowed:** neon glow, bevels, 3-D extrusion, lens flare,
> photography, stock imagery.
> **Never:** shields, padlocks, circuit boards, robot faces, brains, magnifying glasses,
> speedometers, podiums, trophies, stars, rating badges, competitor logos or names in
> the artwork, and any call-to-action button.
> **Required furniture:** eyebrow top-left, UPPERCASE letterspaced white. Thin green
> hairline across the lower third. `AEO ANALYZERS` lower-left and `AEOANALYZERS.COM`
> lower-right, UPPERCASE letterspaced white, small.
> **Text discipline:** every rendered word spelled exactly as written here. Readable at
> thumbnail size. Nothing decorative behind type.

## Production notes

- **Proof every rendered word.** Generators mangle text. If a headline comes back with a
  typo, keep the illustration and set the type yourself — do not re-roll and hope.
- **Export opaque RGB, no alpha channel.** A PNG with transparency renders black-on-black
  in some feeds. Check the channel, do not judge by eye.
- **JPEG quality 95, 4:4:4 chroma** for photographic-weight files; PNG-24 is fine for
  flat vector. Never upscale — generate at or above final size and downscale only.
- **Contrast floor.** White on `#000603` is ~19:1; green `#68E66D` on `#000603` is ~11:1.
  Both clear AAA. Do not tint either toward the background to make it "softer".
- **Check the OG card at 1/4 size** before publishing. If the sub-line is unreadable in a
  feed thumbnail, the card has failed regardless of how it looks at full size.
