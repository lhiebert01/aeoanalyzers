## What makes it different — and why it's better

Most "AI audit" tools hand you a plausible-looking score and some generic advice. Plausible isn't the same as correct, and a smart marketer spots the difference in five seconds. Here's what AEO Analyzers does that we haven't seen done this way anywhere else — each tied to an **outcome**, not a feature.

**1. It never makes you paste a lie into your website.**
Other tools generate JSON-LD schema you're told to paste into your `<head>` — including values they *guessed*. We split every schema into a **"Verified — safe to paste"** block (only values actually found on your page, with the source quote that proves each one) and a **"Candidate — verify first"** block (anything inferred, clearly flagged).
**Outcome:** you never publish a fabricated fact that teaches AI engines something false about your business.

**2. It knows what kind of business you are — and gives the right advice.**
Editorial brand? SaaS landing page? Ecommerce store? Most tools run one generic playbook over everything and tell a carefully-voiced publication to sound like a B2B funnel. We detect your register first and **protect an editorial brand's voice** while giving a SaaS page conversion-focused guidance.
**Outcome:** recommendations that fit your business and don't erase the brand voice that makes you, you.

**3. It tells you the truth about what's missing.**
Other tools flag content as "missing" just because it isn't in the right schema — sending you to write duplicate content you already have. We distinguish *genuinely absent* from *present-but-not-marked-up*, show you the exact on-page sentence, and tell you to **wrap it, not rewrite it.** And it won't recommend creating content for features you don't even offer.
**Outcome:** less busywork, no duplicate content, faster wins your developer can execute verbatim.

**4. It gives you the cure, not just the diagnosis.**
A score is interesting; a fix is valuable. You get **paste-ready JSON-LD, before/after content rewrites, a meta-description rewrite, a prioritized implementation checklist, a downloadable Word report, and a copy-paste handoff template** for your web team.
**Outcome:** you don't just learn you have a problem — you get the exact, deploy-ready solution.

**5. It shows you the invisible competition.**
Gemini 3.5–powered AEO analysis (how Gemini, ChatGPT, and Perplexity each perceive you), a **Citation Probability** score, and a head-to-head **Competitive Duel** against a named rival.
**Outcome:** you finally see where a competitor is winning the citation you should own.

**6. It practices what it sells.**
We ran AEO Analyzers on our *own* site, discovered it was invisible to AI crawlers, and fixed it — our pages are now fully readable and citable by AI engines. We dogfood the exact thing we ask you to do.
**Outcome:** a tool built by people who actually live the problem.

And it does all of this in **90 seconds** — work that would take a web team **hundreds of hours** by hand.
