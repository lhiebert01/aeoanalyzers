A URL, a few minutes, and every transcript behind every number. You decide what to fix, and who fixes it. We do not sell the fix, so the measurement has nothing to prove.

Why this product exists

Buyers now ask AI assistants the questions a business exists to answer, and the engines answer with or without it. AEO Analyzers measures what they say — whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini find a business by name, describe it accurately, and recommend it when buyers ask — reports those apart, each with its own fix path, and backs every figure with a stored transcript. The method is public on the methodology page; the founder runs it on this site and publishes the number every month on the ledger, whatever it is.
