Why this product exists

Buyers now ask AI assistants the questions your business exists to answer, and the engines answer with or without you. Most tools grade your page in isolation and hand you a number. AEO Analyzers measures the outcome that actually matters — whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini recommend you when it counts — and reports it in three separable layers, each with its own fix path, every score backed by a stored transcript. The method is public on the methodology page; the founder runs it on this very site and publishes the number monthly, whatever it is.
