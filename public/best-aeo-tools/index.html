The best AEO tool is not the one with the longest feature list. It is the one that solves the part of the AI visibility problem you actually have.

Most comparisons of AEO software — or AI visibility tools, which is the same category under a different name — count features. That is the wrong place to start.

A business does not buy an AEO platform because it needs another dashboard. It buys one because ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Gemini is failing to find it, describing it incorrectly, citing somebody else, or recommending a competitor. Those are four different problems, and the tools in this category solve different parts of them.

Before you pay for any AEO platform, know the answers to these.

  • Which answer engines actually matter to our buyers?
  • Do we need monitoring, or do we need remediation?
  • Do we care about mentions, citations, factual accuracy, recommendations — or all four?
  • One site, or hundreds?
  • Historical dashboards, or an immediate fix?
  • Can we inspect the evidence behind the score?
  • Can we test whether our changes worked?

If you cannot answer these, no comparison table will pick the right tool for you.

What is answer engine optimization (AEO) software?

AEO software measures whether AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini — mention or recommend a business when someone asks a question, and reports what to change. It is not the same as rank tracking. A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of links; an AEO tool asks a question and reads the answer.

The category is also called GEO, generative engine optimization. See the section below on the difference.

Which tools track AI search visibility?

The tools named most often when we ask AI engines this question, in our own measurement, are Profound, Otterly AI, Peec AI, SE Ranking, AirOps, Scrunch AI, AthenaHQ and Rankscale, alongside broader SEO suites that have added AI-visibility features, including Semrush, Ahrefs, Conductor and HubSpot.

How that list was produced, so you can weigh it. We asked four engines ten unbranded category questions, five times each, in our September 2026 measurement — 200 recorded answers — and counted how many answers mentioned each product at least once. Profound and Otterly AI each appeared in 119, Peec AI in 89, Semrush in 77, Ahrefs in 56, SE Ranking in 45, AirOps in 32, Scrunch AI in 31, AthenaHQ in 29 and Rankscale in 16. AEO Analyzers appeared in 0 of 200. We republish that figure every month, whatever it says. The transcripts are stored and the question set is published.

That is a measurement of what engines say, not of product quality. A tool can be excellent and unnamed, and being named often is partly a function of how long a vendor has been publishing.

How should you compare AEO tools?

By the decision you need the tool to help you make, not by feature count. Every platform on this page sits somewhere on the same loop. Work out which stage is broken for you, and the shortlist picks itself.

OBSERVE → DIAGNOSE → FIX → PROVE → SCALE

DecisionWhat to ask the vendorWhy it decides the purchase
1. ObserveWhich engines, regions and prompts can it actually monitor — and does it distinguish a mention from a link from a citation?"AI visibility" means very different things to different vendors. Engine count alone does not tell you what is being measured.
2. DiagnoseCan it tell you why you are missing, wrong, or losing — separating a retrieval problem from a factual error from a competitive-selection problem?A dashboard reporting that visibility fell does not tell you what caused it, and the three causes have three different fixes.
3. FixDoes it produce changes you can implement — content, schema, technical, source opportunities — or only recommendations?This decides whether you need another tool, a consultant or a manual workflow after the diagnosis.
4. ProveCan you re-ask the real question after the change, and read the answer behind the number?An optimisation score is a hypothesis. A changed answer is an outcome.
5. ScaleHow many brands, prompts, markets, seats and reports do you actually operate?An enterprise team and a single-site operator have genuinely different requirements, and both are legitimate.
6. EconomicsWhat are you paying for — prompts, runs, engines, credits, projects, countries or seats?Two plans at the same monthly price can deliver radically different usable measurement volume.

What counts as success? Six different answers

A platform might call all six of these "visibility", and they are not the same outcome:

  • The AI knows your company exists.
  • The AI mentions your company.
  • The AI links to your site.
  • The AI cites your page as a source.
  • The AI describes your company accurately.
  • The AI recommends you for an unbranded buying question.

Decide which of those you are buying before you compare prices. It is the reason we report retrievability, fidelity and citation win as three separate numbers rather than one — a business can be perfectly findable by name and never recommended, and that is a different problem with a different fix.

The value is the short path from problem to fix to evidence.

Monitoring tells you where you stand. Diagnosis tells you why. Remediation tells you what to change. Verification tells you whether it worked. Know which of those four you are buying.

We will not tell you that other platforms only monitor while we fix. That is no longer true of this category and it was never a good argument.

Where we actually sit. Many platforms here are optimised for ongoing visibility intelligence — tracking brands, competitors, prompts and citations across large datasets, at a scale we do not serve. AEO Analyzers starts from a different question: what is preventing this site from being retrieved correctly, described accurately, or selected for the question — and what should change?

So it separates three outcomes: retrievability (can the engine find you when asked), fidelity (does it describe you correctly), and citation win (does it select you when the buyer never says your name) — then connects each to a site diagnosis, a fix, and a re-test. That suits a buyer who does not want another dashboard and does want to know what is wrong, what to change, and whether it worked. It suits a forty-brand portfolio operator considerably less well, and we would rather say so here than in month two.

The principle, if you take nothing else from this page: do not choose an AEO platform by counting features. Choose it by working out which decisions it can help you make — and whether it can show you the evidence behind them. The questions to ask are in the AEO Buyer’s Standard.

One method that works on any vendor here, including us: ask for the raw answers behind a score, not just the score. If a tool reports that you appear in 34% of answers, ask which answers, from which engine, on which date, how many repetitions, and whether you can read them. Numbers from different vendors are not comparable without that, because each asks different questions a different number of times on different days — and an engine's answer varies between runs.

AEO, GEO, AI visibility tools, AI SEO tools — what is the difference?

In practice the terms are used interchangeably by most vendors, and the distinction is more editorial than technical. Answer engine optimization tends to emphasise being the answer to a direct question; generative engine optimization tends to emphasise appearing inside generated content of any kind. If a vendor draws a sharp line between them, ask what they measure differently as a result.

The same is true of the other names this category trades under. AI visibility tools, AI SEO tools, AI search monitoring, LLMO and AIO all describe the same job: finding out whether an answer engine names your business, and changing the result. We use AEO because it names the thing being optimised — the answer — but a page calling itself an AI visibility tool is not selling you something different. The noun varies too: people search for an AEO tool, AEO software, an AEO platform and an AEO analyzer, and mean the same thing. Our own search console records people arriving on the third and fourth of those.

Worth knowing if you are searching for one of these: when we asked four engines this question in our September 2026 measurement, the pages they cited back were titled “best AI SEO tools” and “best AI visibility tools” far more often than “AEO tools”. The vocabulary the engines retrieve is not yet the vocabulary the category uses about itself.

How can I check my own brand right now?

You do not need a tool for the first look. Open ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini, ask each the question a buyer would ask — not your brand name, the category — and write down which companies get named. Do it three times per engine, because the answers vary. That is the whole method; the tools automate it, add repetition, and keep the receipts.

If you want that automated, our free check reports the diagnosis without an account. We published our own result: on those category questions, AI recommended us 0% of the time across 200 recorded answers, and we wrote up the zero rather than quietly fixing it.

One test before paying for any of them — including ours

Ask the tool to do three things:

  1. Show you a real AI answer.
  2. Explain why that result matters for your business.
  3. Name one concrete thing you can change because of it.

If it cannot do all three, ask what exactly the dashboard is measuring. In answer engine optimisation, measurement is not the objective. Becoming the answer is.
