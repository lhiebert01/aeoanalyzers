The product, in plain terms

What it is
An Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) tool that measures how AI assistants describe, rank, and cite a brand — and returns the exact fixes.
What it measures
Three separable layers: retrievability (are you found by name), fidelity (are you described correctly), and citation win (are you recommended on unbranded buyer questions). See the methodology.
How
It queries the real engines — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini — with branded and category questions, several times each, and stores every transcript.
Who it's for
Marketing teams, SEO/AEO agencies, SaaS founders, publishers, and ecommerce brands who need to know — and improve — how AI assistants represent them.
Pricing
