1. Create your Wikidata entries (about 30 minutes)

Where to start: https://www.wikidata.org (create a free account with "Create account", top right)

  1. Create a free Wikidata account and sign in.
  2. Search Wikidata for your company and your product first. If an item already exists, edit it instead of creating a second one.
  3. Open https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:NewItem. Label: Your company. Description: short and neutral, such as "software company in Your City, Country". Aliases: other spellings people use.
  4. Add each statement in the company table: click "+ add statement", type the property, pick it from the list, type the value, pick it, then "+ add reference", choose "reference URL" and paste the source. Publish.
  5. Create the product item the same way (Label: Your product) with the product table, then link the two: "product or material produced" on the company, "developer" on the product.
  6. When each directory listing goes live, add it to the item as a "described at URL" statement with itself as the reference.

Wikidata keeps entries only when independent sources back them up, so list on directories first (Part 2) and add each live listing as a reference afterward.

Descriptions must be short and neutral, never promotional.

Wikipedia is different: it does not accept articles a company writes about itself.

Done when: Both items have their own Q-number (wikidata.org/wiki/Q…), and every statement has a reference.
