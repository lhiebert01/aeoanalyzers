Why the fixes ship faster than the number moves

The fixes I control are the on-site, first-party layer: what my own pages say and how machine-readable they are. Those go live the day I deploy them. But category citation win is decided mostly off-site — by whether the sources the engines already trust (the roundups, the review sites, the communities) have caught up to include me. That corroboration accrues on the internet's clock, not mine. The engines also carry forward what they learned before my fixes; a month is often not enough for a re-crawl and re-weighting to show up in the answer.

This is the lag every business faces. You can do everything right on your own property and still wait, because the layer that decides the recommendation isn't your property — it's the corroborating record around you. Anyone selling you a same-week guarantee is selling the part they don't control.
