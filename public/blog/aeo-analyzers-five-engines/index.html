AEO Analyzers Expands AI Visibility Measurement to Five Answer Engines

Website diagnostics identify improvements while Citation Sweeps record how AI engines describe, recommend and cite businesses in tested answers.

December 8, 2026 · PIGENAI LLC

KANSAS CITY, MO, UNITED STATES, December 8, 2026. PIGENAI LLC today announced expanded AI visibility measurement in AEO Analyzers, its answer engine optimization platform. Businesses can examine how five AI answer engines describe their brand, whether those engines recommend it in response to category questions, and which sources they cite.

When a buyer asks AI for a recommendation, does it name your business? Being known by name is not the same as being recommended to a prospective customer.

AEO Analyzers brings website diagnostics and direct tests of AI answers together. The platform is available at https://aeoanalyzers.com.

Two Measurements, Two Different Questions

The free AEO Score examines a website for structure, content density, clarity and entity signals. It provides a score, sub-scores and specific gaps. It is a diagnostic assessment, not a measurement of actual recommendations.

The paid Citation Sweep asks AI answer engines the questions a prospective buyer might ask and keeps the transcript of every answer. The sweep measures three separate outcomes.

Retrievability: When asked about your business by name, does the engine find and name your website?

Fidelity: Does the answer match key facts on your website, such as your company name and founders, or does it confuse you with another business of the same name?

Citation Win: Does the engine recommend your business when asked about your category without naming your brand, and which businesses or sources does it cite instead?

Pro and Business plans ask five answer engines: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Grok. The no-subscription Day Pass asks four. Every sweep report names the engines it asked.

See the Evidence Before You Spend

A Citation Sweep keeps the questions and answers behind its results. Businesses and agencies can read the transcripts and the cited sources before deciding where to invest in content, directories, public relations or other marketing. A citation shows what was observed in a tested answer. It is not a guarantee of future visibility or sales.

From Diagnosis to Action

Paid access provides structured data suggestions, content rewrites, a prioritized checklist and a Word report. Suggested structured data separates verified information from assumptions that need review. Customers decide which improvements to make and who makes them.

Repeated sweeps let users compare dated observations. Changes in model behavior, question wording and other conditions can affect results, so a comparison alone does not establish that a particular website change caused an improvement.

"A website score tells you what to investigate. A Citation Sweep shows what AI actually said in the tests," said Lindsay Hiebert, CISSP, Founder and CEO of PIGENAI LLC. "Businesses deserve to see the evidence before deciding what to change or where to spend their marketing budget."

Built for Business Owners and Agencies

AEO Analyzers is designed for businesses, marketing agencies, online stores, developers and founders who want to understand their visibility in AI answers. Agencies can use the recorded responses to explain findings to clients and prioritize work.
