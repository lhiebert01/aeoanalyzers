Methodology

How AEO Analyzers measures

We report three separable layers of answer-engine visibility — each with its own fix path — and every score is backed by a stored transcript you can reproduce. This page is the full method: what we ask, how many times, how we count, and what we deliberately refuse to claim.

Last reviewed August 2026 · question panel v1.1

The three layers

A single blended "visibility score" hides more than it shows. Being known by name is not the same as being described correctly, and neither is the same as being recommended to a buyer. We separate them, because the fix for each is different.

1 · Retrievability

When someone asks the engines about you by name, do they find and describe you at all? This is a branded question set.

If it's low: crawler access, indexable structure, a machine-readable record of who you are.
2 · Fidelity

When the engines describe you, are they right? Every branded answer is checked against your site's own record and classified accurate, drifted, or confused with a named colliding entity — with that entity named.

If it drifts: correct the source of record, disambiguate against the lookalikes that share your letters.
3 · Citation Win

On unbranded buyer questions — "what tools measure AI search visibility?" — do the engines recommend you, or a competitor by name? This is the category question set.

If it's zero: third-party corroboration and content shaped like the questions buyers actually ask — not more copy on your own domain.

The question panel

Each run uses a fixed panel of branded and category questions written in natural buyer phrasing — the way a real person asks an assistant, not keyword strings. The panel is versioned (currently v1.1) and disclosed: when we change a question, we record the change and the version, so month-over-month numbers compare like with like. Same questions, same way, every time — that discipline is what makes the trend line honest.

Repetition, sample size, and confidence

Engines are non-deterministic — ask twice, get two answers — so a single query proves nothing. Every question is asked several times per engine (the series baseline uses five), across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. Every number we report ships with its sample size (N) and a confidence read printed next to it. A result at N=8 is not presented as if it were N=200.

Measurement integrity

We built these guards after finding our own instrument getting them wrong:

  • Truncation is excluded, not counted as a loss. If an engine's answer is cut off before it finishes, that run is flagged and removed — never silently scored as "didn't cite you."
  • Search-grounded and model-prior answers are scored separately. An engine answering from memory without searching the web is a different measurement from one that actually retrieved sources; we don't blend them.
  • Segment-aware. A zero on "best AEO tool for enterprise" is out-of-segment for a self-serve SMB tool, not a failure — we don't score you against questions that were never yours to win.

Fixes are tiered by attainability

Plenty of visibility advice ends the same way: "become more authoritative — get on Wikipedia, get into the big publications." For most businesses that's a goose chase. Every source we suggest is tiered: what you can do yourself this week, what's genuinely pitchable, and what's aspirational — labeled as aspirational, not disguised as a to-do. Your time is the scarcest input in your business; an action plan should spend it where movement is actually possible.

Reproducibility and cadence

Every claim is backed by a stored transcript — the exact question, the exact answer, the engine, the timestamp — that you can reproduce yourself. We re-measure on a monthly cadence, the same questions the same way, and publish the number each time, whatever it is.

What we do not claim

We do not know the engines' ranking algorithms, and we don't pretend to. We measure what they actually do when asked your buyers' questions — the observed output, not the black box. We make no guarantee of rankings or citations; anyone who promises that is guessing. What we offer is an honest, dated, reproducible measurement and a fix path scoped to what you can reach.
