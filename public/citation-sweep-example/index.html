A Citation Sweep example, question by question

One stored sweep of aeoanalyzers.com itself, shown with its questions, engines, models, classifications and a full answer.

What was run

  • Site measured: aeoanalyzers.com (our own site).
  • Run: October 13, 2026, at 8:34 PM US Central time (01:34 UTC on October 14).
  • Panel: v1.2 (the Pro and Business panel), five answer engines.
  • Each question was asked 5 times on each engine.
  • Questions shown here: the two questions that name the site: “who is aeoanalyzers.com”, “what is aeoanalyzers.com”.

Engines and models

  • Claude, model claude-haiku-4-5
  • ChatGPT, model gpt-4o
  • Perplexity, model sonar
  • Gemini, model gemini-3.6-flash
  • Grok, model grok-4.7

One full stored answer

Gemini, model gemini-3.6-flash, question “who is aeoanalyzers.com”, answer 5 of 5. The text below is the stored answer, unedited.

aeoanalyzers.com is an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) analytics platform developed by PIGENAI LLC and founded by Lindsay Hiebert. The tool measures a website's brand visibility and citability across major AI answer engines, including ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Perplexity (perplexity.ai), and Google Gemini (gemini.google.com). It audits content and structured schema markup to provide actionable recommendations for improving AI search engine recommendations.

Limits of this example

This is one sweep on one date. Answers change as the engines change, so another run can read differently.

The fidelity check compares an answer with the facts recorded from the site: the company, the founder and the stated facts. It does not check every detail. Several stored answers in this sweep quote plan prices from an earlier version of the pricing page, and the check did not flag them.

The sweep also asked category questions that do not name the site. Those questions and answers name other products, so they are not reproduced here. The monthly results for category questions are published on the Honest-Zero ledger.

A sweep records what was observed in tested answers. It is not a guarantee of future visibility.
